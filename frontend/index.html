Science born from necessity.

PBD Project was founded by Andrew Longenecker after his son Diego was diagnosed with PEX10 Zellweger Spectrum Disorder — a rare peroxisomal biogenesis disorder affecting fewer than 1 in 50,000 people. What Andrew discovered wasn't just a disease. It was a field of biology with thousands of published papers, critical unanswered questions, and no unified way to search across them.

PeroxiOS is the answer to that gap. Built in collaboration with rare disease AI researchers including Adam Freygang (creator of ARIA, the gene-based RAG platform), PeroxiOS structures peroxisome knowledge into a queryable engine — so researchers, clinicians, biopharma scientists, and families can find what matters faster. Every query cites its sources. Every answer is traceable.

What PeroxiOS knows.

Four domains of peroxisome biology, covered by a growing open-access corpus.

Peroxisomal Biogenesis

PEX gene function, import pathways, biogenesis mechanisms.

Zellweger Spectrum Disorders

Clinical phenotypes, genotype-phenotype correlations, natural history.

Metabolic Pathways

Fatty acid β-oxidation, plasmalogen synthesis, ROS metabolism.

Therapeutic Research

Gene therapy approaches, small molecule candidates, model systems.

Corpus: ~500 curated PMC open-access papers (Phase 1 pilot). Growing continuously.
